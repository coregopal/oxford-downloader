## 💧 LESSON 6: पाणी वाचवा रे ! (Save Water!)

### 📖 English Summary
This chapter is a beautiful, educational poem that teaches children the vital importance of water conservation. It explains that water is precious and we must not waste even a single drop, as lakes are formed drop-by-drop. It guides us to use only as much water as needed for daily chores like washing feet and bathing. Storing rainwater in household tanks and building dams helps secure a constant water supply and relieves us of future worries. 

### 📌 Important Points
1. **Drop-by-Drop Importance:** Small drops of water accumulate to form a massive lake. Every small effort counts in conservation. [33, 47]
2. **Mindful Usage:** While washing hands/feet or bathing, we should use water very sparingly and turn off taps immediately. [33, 48]
3. **Storage & Infrastructure:** Rainwater should be collected in household water tanks (हौद) and larger dams (धरण) to eliminate water scarcity and tension. [33, 48]

### 🗣️ Vocabulary (शब्दार्थ) with Meanings and Pronunciation [33, 47]
1. **बचत (Bachat):** savings / conservation
2. **पाणी (Pani):** water
3. **थेंब (Themb):** drop
4. **तळे (Tale):** lake / pond
5. **आंघोळ (Anghol):** bath
6. **हौद (Haud):** water tank
7. **धरण (Dharan):** dam
8. **काळजी (Kalji):** worry / anxiety
9. **सांडणे (Sandne):** to spill / to waste

## 🕉️ LESSON 8: संत तुकाराम (Saint Tukaram)

### 📖 English Summary
This chapter introduces students to the legendary Marathi saint, Saint Tukaram Maharaj. He is highly revered across Maharashtra for initiating massive social awakening through his simple, soul-stirring devotional poems called 'Abhangas'. Despite facing tremendous personal hardships—including losing his beloved son Santu and his cattle to a severe, tragic famine (दुष्काळ)—his absolute devotion to Lord Vitthal remained unshaken. Warkaris call him 'Jagadguru' with profound respect.

### 📌 Important Points
1. **Social Contribution:** Saints used the power of 'Abhangas' to generate critical social awareness, moral values, and spiritual vigilance. [39, 55]
2. **Personal Life:** Saint Tukaram was born into a family with high values. His full name was Tukaram Bolhoba Ambile. [39, 56]
3. **Spiritual Title:** Due to his immense spiritual wisdom and poetry, followers of the Warkari sect called him "Jagadguru" (Teacher of the World). [39, 56]
4. **Suffering & Resilience:** Tukaram Maharaj faced severe grief. A deadly drought claimed the life of his eldest son, Santu, and destroyed his household livelihood, yet his faith never wavered. [39, 40, 57]

### 🗣️ Vocabulary (शब्दार्थ) with Meanings and Pronunciation [38, 39, 55]
1. **जागरूकता (Jagarukta):** vigilance / public awareness
2. **जन्म (Janma):** birth
3. **जबाबदारी (Jababdari):** responsibility
4. **दुःख (Dukha):** sorrow / grief
5. **तीर्थाटनाला (Tirthatnala):** to a pilgrimage
6. **भयंकर (Bhayankar):** extreme / terrifying
7. **सामना (Samna):** confrontation / to face difficulties
8. **भक्ती (Bhakti):** pure devotion
9. **दुष्काळ (Dushkal):** drought / famine

* **४. त्याचे अंग रंगीबेरंगी आणि गळा निळ्या रंगाचा असतो.**
  * *Pronunciation:* Tyache ang rangiberangi ani gala nilya rangacha asto.
  * *Translation:* Its body is colorful and its neck is blue in color.
* **५. मोराला लांब आणि अतिशय सुंदर पिसारा असतो.**
  * *Pronunciation:* Morala lamb ani atishay sundar pisara asto.
  * *Translation:* The peacock has long and extremely beautiful feathers.
* **६. पाऊस पडताना मोर आपला पिसारा फुलवून थुईथुई नाचतो.**
  * *Pronunciation:* Paus padtana mor apla pisara phulvun thuithui nachto.
  * *Translation:* When it rains, the peacock spreads its feathers and dances joyfully.
* **७. मोर हा भारताचा राष्ट्रीय पक्षी आहे.**
  * *Pronunciation:* Mor ha bhartacha rashtriya pakshi ahe.
  * *Translation:* Peacock is the national bird of India.
* **८. असा हा देखणा मोर मला खूप आवडतो.**
  * *Pronunciation:* Asa ha dekhna mor mala khup avadto.
  * *Translation:* I like this handsome peacock very much.
